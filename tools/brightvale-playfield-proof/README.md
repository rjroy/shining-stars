# Brightvale playfield geometry proof

Run `./tools/brightvale-playfield-proof/validate.sh` from the repository root. The script owns and stops its Vite server, loads the production platform/highway/endcaps and checks their hashes, captures Chromium reference and narrow-desktop views as WebP, and writes the measured JSON reports here. It uses installed Three.js, Vite, Chromium, Python, and the checked-in GLBs/layout only; no production asset or live game scene is modified.

## Coordinate contract

The four lane centers and actual luminous target centers remain the logical judging frame. `point(lane, progress)` is the unoffset logical coordinate (`progress=1` equals `hitAnchor`); `visualPoint` is the separate rendering pose. It places an 0.08-unit-thick note body above the measured mesh surface by 0.055 authored units. This deliberately does not move the scoring anchor. The local-Y ray direction is transformed through the exact shared 14° parent matrix, so every ray holds its local lane/Z fixed; reports include each inverse-transformed hit Z. The real loaded geometry measures all four travel lanes at 2.75 units and target centers within 0.00000005 units of the logical anchors.

At 673 stations per lane across the full 42-unit run, the proof casts local-vertical rays at three lateral positions (center and ±1.0): 8,076 actual geometry rays total. They build the measured footprint ceiling. Additional actual-mesh ray checks sample each difficulty envelope at 169 progress points and three lateral positions through the physical road extent. The timing envelope is built from real 2.1×0.08-unit box segments with depth testing enabled; each segment follows the measured visual profile. The reports record a 0.055-unit minimum underside gap for Easy, Normal, and Hard, with 252 successful surface checks for each. Outside the physical runway after progress 1, the visual path carries the terminal surface height but continues along unbounded progress; there is no claim of road clearance past the loaded mesh. The complete measured path has 673 increasing world-Z samples; the smallest step is 0.0494656 units in both reports.

## Camera and evidence

Both captures preserve the approved 39.76070473° FOV and view direction. The camera is pulled back 1.5× along the center-hit ray from `(0, 13.9, 15)` to keep the Easy late timing endpoint in frame. At 1600×900, hit centers are about x=572, 724, 876, 1028 and y=584. The narrow evidence case uses a 1120×700 host with a centered 896×568 scene viewport and 66px persistent HUD reservations at top and bottom; target centers and all difficulty endpoints fit. This is one proven viewport sample, **not a declared minimum supported viewport** or final integrated-game camera. Establishing a minimum is deferred to full composition and device acceptance, rather than inferred from this isolated proof.

The screenshots and reports are Chromium/SwiftShader visual and geometry evidence, not hardware-performance or physics/collision guarantees. The isolated proof uses illustrative note/envelope geometry and colors, not the production note renderer, labels, or complete HUD. The 0.055-unit gap and 2.1-unit note width are bounded proof geometry decisions; final art and camera fit should be reviewed when the real renderer/HUD is integrated.
