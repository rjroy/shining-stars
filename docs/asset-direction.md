# Brightvale asset direction

This is the project-specific entry point for future asset briefs. It summarizes
approved visual direction and points to the evidence; it is not authorization or
approval for an individual asset. For generic production workflow and approval
gates, use the configured asset-development plugin. The project-specific
[asset production record](asset-production.md) collects maintained package
locations and historical production evidence.

## Approved visual principles

- Use a low, dramatic viewpoint and centered highway dominant in the foreground;
  city and crowd frame it but remain subordinate. Keep four lanes readable and
  notes compact, crisp-edged bars with restrained halos.
- Preserve the perpetual-twilight distinction: warm, welcoming Audience versus
  violet/darker Twilight; these are not day and night. Gameplay cues must not
  rely on color alone.
- Protect lane width and readable playfield first on narrow layouts; crop side
  scenery before compressing lanes. These are priorities, not validated
  responsive-fit results.
- Do not import mockup decoration or change controls, HUD, scoring, timing, or
  mechanics as a consequence of asset direction. Detailed timing/projection
  discussion in the approved direction document is a proposal and review
  contract, not validated geometry or implementation.

For each asset, cite its actual approved brief/concept and current references;
the direction above does not approve a specific silhouette, material, concept,
model, derivative, or runtime appearance. See the [production examples and
their status](asset-production.md) before reusing historical results.
