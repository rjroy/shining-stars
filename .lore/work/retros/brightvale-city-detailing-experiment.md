# Brightvale city detailing experiment

## Result and user assessment

The user accepted the complete native city scene after the bounded residual-interface finishing pass, replying **“great”** after the right-side approach and water/contact caveats were disclosed. The approved version is [`candidate.blend`](../../../assets/experiments/brightvale-city/finishing/model/candidate.blend), SHA-256 `edec71ce0b04189bcee6d7f98d14ad057be37ad6758bba9524a333819a34caff`. The [finishing record](../../../assets/experiments/brightvale-city/finishing/model/README.md), [whole-city review sheet](../../../assets/experiments/brightvale-city/finishing/model/previews/review-sheet.png), and [coverage table](../../../assets/experiments/brightvale-city/finishing/model/coverage.md) describe the saved scene and evidence. This is native-scene approval, not production approval, game integration, or runtime acceptance.

The user had earlier rated the tower experiment **A−** against the previous **C+** attempt. For Brightvale, the user accepted the complete blockout as a vast improvement, said the quality was undeniable while noting it was expensive (“lots of Astra”), and then accepted the detailed final scene. These are the user's assessments. No token count, dollar amount, elapsed time, or controlled causal comparison was measured.

## Scope, authority, and construction

The approved city breakdown retained eight families: citadel; paired districts; receding skyline; terraced cliffs/retaining walls; bridges; watercourses/waterfalls; vegetation; and shared lighting/atmosphere. An earlier city baseline excluded water and foliage even though both appeared in the reference. The user rejected that scope reduction. Concepts were made for connected assemblies; their authority was scoped by panel/content. The first skyline sheet was rejected because it presented replacement-like full scenes instead of isolated subordinate groups; a corrected sheet followed. Lighting references were authorized for lighting direction only because generated architecture and foliage drifted.

The composition map and full-city blockout preceded detailed assembly work. The user clarified that concept art was direction rather than a pixel contract, accepted the blockout improvement, and authorized only bounded corrections before detail. Modeling proceeded by connected scope: citadel, ravine crossing, paired districts, skyline, remaining landscape, then residual banks and secondary bridges. The editable `.blend` remained the primary artifact. Work used incremental Blender background-Python edits to saved scenes, actual renders, saved checkpoints, and inspection; there was no live GUI modeling session and no full-scene regeneration guarantee.

Each later package used a preserved accepted source, named ownership, actual views, and structural checks. The landscape pass recorded remaining coarse bank spurs and secondary crossings rather than calling them complete. The final finishing pass accounted for all 13 bank spurs and 79 secondary-crossing components. Two fully buried bank spurs were retained unchanged; eleven visible caps received restrained treatment. A channel-bed wedge required a specifically named local exception to the protected landscape interface. The finishing README records the exact source chain, protected objects, edits, evidence, and checks.

The user accepted successive bounded stages and gave three final targeted blockout corrections: improve the arch read-through, subdivide central roof masses, and deepen the lower terrain/water/planting. The final finishing review disclosed the inherited narrow/raised right approach and remaining water/stair/sidebank contacts. Checks reported 8/8 handoff tests passing, successful saved-scene validation, and protected-data/interface checks within their stated scope; these checks were not treated as artistic or runtime approval.

## Remaining limits at acceptance

The scene uses stylized static water and provisional Blender units. The right approach remains narrow/raised in part; inherited stair, sidebank, and downstream river contacts remain outside the explicitly corrected interface. No complete route walkability, exhaustive watertight union, physical/structural/hydrological certification, or collision-free city claim was made. No runtime validation, production derivative, optimization, export, or integration occurred. Acceptance applies to this scene/version and does not grant unlimited further work.

## Boundary of this record

This record describes the observed experiment and the user's stated evaluations. It does not establish that cost caused quality, that incremental modeling is universally superior, or that the scene is ready for a game. The separate [workflow proposal](../design/iterative-asset-workflow-proposal.md) interprets these observations and proposes generic skill changes; those changes have not been applied.
