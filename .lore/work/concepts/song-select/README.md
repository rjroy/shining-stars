## Observatory: 100+ song navigation refinement

On 2026-10-14, the user selected the Observatory direction and approved its narrative: the player is the performer, channeling music to save the world's future. The user explicitly approved the exact versions of both screens listed below on **2026-10-14**. This approval covers these visual concepts only. The “100+ songs,” collection sizes, song names/art, and any best/result values remain illustrative—not actual catalog or player data—and do not settle scoring, unlocks, or progression. Approval does not authorize implementation. The screens extend the original `twilight-observatory.png` without replacing or overwriting it.

The proposed flow: Screen 1 presents several large, readable constellation collections, while persistent **All Songs**, search, Favorites, and Filters provide direct routes that do not depend on locating small stars. Selecting a collection opens Screen 2, with a breadcrumb/back route, a visible scrollable song list, selected-song details, difficulty choice, and Play. Screen 2 also keeps All Songs, search, Favorites, and Filters close at hand. This is a concept for handling scale, not a settled interaction specification. The “100+ songs,” collection sizes, constellations, song names/art, and any best/result values are illustrative only; they are not actual catalog or player data. No Audience/Twilight selector is intended.
