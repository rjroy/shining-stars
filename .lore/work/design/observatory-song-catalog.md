# Observatory song catalog and selection contract

## Purpose and authority

**2026-10-19 progression-filter amendment (shining-stars-0mv):** The current filter panel adds five independent controls: Show Unlocked; Show Unplayed; Undefeated on difficulty (None/Easy/Normal/Hard, default None); Less than 5 normal stars; Less than 5 golden stars. These are separate controls, not a combined category dropdown. Unchecked/None means no condition. Every active condition intersects with search, collection, Favorites, and difficulty support. Unplayed means no saved rating: a completed zero-star defeat is played. Stars use the existing best-across-difficulty rating; unplayed qualifies for both below-five conditions, while five normal stars or any gold stars excludes below-five-normal. Undefeated compares the highest credited victory (Normal includes Easy; Hard includes all tiers), independently of run difficulty or chart support. Show Unlocked uses actual progression access, including the starting collection; otherwise progression-locked songs remain inspectable and favoritable. Recompute from current save data after results, unlocks, imports, and reset, reconciling selected ID and run difficulty if results change. No save schema or persistent filter state is added. This amendment supersedes the original prohibition on progression filters below and the b6f note that this work was still separate; the b6f hide-unavailable policy remains in force.

**2026-10-19 availability amendment (shining-stars-b6f):** Editorially unavailable songs are now hidden from every player catalog route, including collections, search, and saved Favorites. The availability filter is removed; difficulty support remains independent of run difficulty, and the remaining browse filters still intersect. Collection and result counts reflect visible entries only. The catalog's editorial availability field and playback safety checks remain intact. Progression-locked songs remain visible, inspectable, and favoritable with the existing collection/song unlock flow; editorial availability is not a progression lock. This amendment supersedes the original unavailable-row and availability-filter behavior below. No progression filters are introduced (separate shining-stars-0mv).

Define the smallest reliable contract for a pre-game song browser that can grow beyond 100 songs without making the constellation map the only way to find a song. **Design approved by the user on 2026-10-14; implementation planning is next.** No runtime implementation has been made or authorized by this document approval.

**Approved direction:** the player is the performer using music to save Brightvale's future. The exact approved screens are the constellation overview and song browser listed above. Preserve their All Songs, search, Favorites, Filters, collection breadcrumb/back path, scrollable song list, selected-song detail, difficulty, and Play affordances. The approved baseline behavior and architecture are recorded below; the images themselves do not settle unlisted data or mechanics. Screen labels such as “100+ songs,” sample titles, scores, and constellation names are illustrative. The original five concept candidates remain unapproved.

**Current facts and approved baseline:** seven songs are present in `data/charts/song-authoring.json`; six have full-track `.candidate.ts` charts that are generated and not human-verified. The user approved an initial single constellation containing all seven songs, alongside direct All Songs and search paths. Do not invent its name or story; its title awaits curation. The six generated candidates may be played while they remain candidates and are refined; candidate status is not itself an availability gate. Favorites are stored locally in this browser. First Shining Star remains an explicitly labeled 65.8-second demo excerpt (`sourceStart: 107.1`, authored excerpt-relative Twilight boundary 39.2); its chart in `src/game/mvp-chart.ts` is also generated and not human-verified. Full-song First Shining Star is settled product intent; authoring and verifying the full-song chart is follow-up `.4f4.7.6`, not a question about product scope. Do not claim the current excerpt is full-song or verified. Score history, unlocks, and progression rules remain deferred.

## Approved architecture

Use one explicit, statically imported catalog registry as the source for selection metadata and song-to-audio/chart pairing. Keep chart note data in the existing typed chart files; each registry record points directly to one chart and one bundled audio URL. This is enough for 100+ records and avoids a plugin system, runtime file discovery, or inferred metadata. `main.ts` currently binds the MVP chart and audio directly; later work should resolve one selected catalog record before constructing the existing controller/audio/game flow.

The registry should contain:

| Field          | Contract                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`           | Required stable, unique, machine-safe ID (for example a slug); independent of title, filename, list position, and translated copy. Never recycle an ID for a different song.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `title`        | Required user-facing title. Do not derive or embellish it from filenames.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `sortOrder`    | Required authored integer for the default All Songs order. Sort ascending; break ties by normalized title, then ID, so ordering stays deterministic.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `audioUrl`     | Required statically bundled URL for the recording paired with this chart. Do not construct a path from user-facing title or accept arbitrary remote URLs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `chart`        | Required typed `ChartConfig`; it remains authoritative for allowed difficulties, `sourceStart`, selected-segment `duration`, Twilight boundary, and notes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `segmentKind`  | Required authored `excerpt` or `full` declaration. It does not replace chart timing: `ChartConfig.sourceStart` and `duration` remain exact, with no automatic timing adjustment. The current transport accepts decoded audio only when its duration is at least `sourceStart + duration`. Full tracks must have zero source offset. Before integration, measure actual decoded durations and document positive decoded-minus-chart residuals across supported browsers; distinguish harmless metadata/sample rounding or MP3 padding from omitted song content. Do not invent a numerical tolerance here. |
| `availability` | Required explicit `available` or `unavailable` editorial state. This is not an unlock calculation or account entitlement. Unavailable entries may be shown with an unavailable indication, but cannot start. No lock reason or unlock rule is implied.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `chartStatus`  | Required `candidate` or `verified` authoring-confidence state, independent from availability. Candidate means not human-verified; it does not mean structurally invalid or block play by itself. Structural validation still runs before a song can start. Do not present candidate status as a score, lock, or player achievement.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `collections`  | Explicit ordered IDs of curated collections, or empty pending curation. Never infer collection/story membership from title, filename, genre, audio, or chart data.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

The registry must validate unique IDs, deterministic ordering, non-empty titles, valid chart data, non-empty supported difficulties, a valid segment declaration, paired audio/chart references, known collection IDs, and that the default difficulty rule produces a supported choice. Keep any authored collection records (stable ID, title, order, member IDs) explicit and validate references in both directions. Do not store duplicate timing fields in the catalog: use `ChartConfig.sourceStart` and `duration` as the timing source of truth. Preserve all six current full-track candidates' authored `twilightAt === duration / 2` values exactly; do not normalize or recompute them. Preserve First Shining Star's existing excerpt boundary exactly until its separately authored full-song chart replaces it.

Use the existing difficulty set (`easy`, `normal`, `hard`) and `defaultDifficulty()` policy: choose Normal when supported, otherwise the first authored allowed difficulty. Only expose difficulties listed by the selected chart. Keep the difficulty filter as independent browser state: choosing “songs supporting Hard” does not select Hard for a run, and selecting a song does not change the filter. When a song is selected, retain the previous run difficulty if that chart supports it; otherwise choose that song's `defaultDifficulty()`. Freeze the selected run difficulty into the started run; catalog or filter changes must not mutate an active run.

## Navigation and selection behavior

1. **Constellation overview:** the initial map contains one constellation with all seven current songs; its display name and story are not invented here. Preserve the approved selected-collection detail panel and its explicit **Browse Collection** action: selecting the constellation updates that panel; activating Browse Collection opens the song browser scoped to it. Keep All Songs, search, Favorites, and Filters directly reachable. A user must be able to reach every listed song without finding a small constellation. More collections may be added only through explicit curation.
2. **Collection browser:** selecting a constellation on the overview updates its detail panel; only activating Browse Collection opens the list, scoped to that collection, with a clear breadcrumb and back action. All Songs opens the same list without a collection constraint. Search, favorites, and filters remain available on either path.
3. **All Songs:** list every catalog entry in stable authored order, including unavailable entries with a clear non-startable state. Do not silently omit entries because chart status is candidate. Candidate and playable are different from availability.
4. **Search:** case-insensitive substring match against title only; normalize Unicode and accents for matching. Apply search within the current collection/list scope. No undocumented genre, artist, or story fields are searchable.
5. **Favorites:** toggle a per-song favorite keyed by stable song ID. Recommended storage is local to this browser/device (for example local storage), with no account sync implied. Favorites view is a filter over All Songs; combining it with search or other filters intersects results.
6. **Filters:** offer only known, useful facets: availability and explicit “supports difficulty” filters independent from the run difficulty. Favorites is a separate shortcut but composes as a filter. Do not add genre, mood, rank, unlock, or “recommended” filters without authored data and an approved rule.
7. **Song selection and reconciliation:** selecting a row updates the detail panel and the selected stable ID, not just its visible row index. Keep selection while it remains in results. On filter/search change, first compute results; then keep the selected ID if it remains, otherwise select the first available result in stable catalog order, or clear selection if there is none. Finally reconcile run difficulty: keep the previous run choice if supported by the resulting selected song, otherwise use `defaultDifficulty()` for that song; with no selection, there is no run difficulty and Play is disabled. Filter changes never implicitly select a run difficulty. Returning from gameplay restores the same song/list context when still valid; exact lifecycle transitions belong to `.7.4`.
8. **Difficulty and Play:** show the chosen song's supported difficulty options beside its Play action. Play is enabled only when a song is selected, explicitly available, its chart passes structural validation, and the selected difficulty is supported. Resolve the same record's audio and chart together; never fall back to another song or to the MVP chart on a missing reference. Audio load failure stays attached to that selection, offers retry or return to browsing, and does not silently change the selected song.

The initial single constellation is an approved organizational choice, not a claim that the seven titles share an inferred theme. Leave its name and story copy for curation; do not derive either from titles. Future collection placement and naming remain editorial work, and never replace direct All Songs access.

## UI, audio, and rendering boundary

Use a deliberate hybrid composition: Three.js renders the Observatory's celestial environment and visual effects; styled HTML/SVG provides constellation selection and the readable, accessible controls, including search, lists, details, filters, difficulty, and Play. Compose these layers as one intentional Observatory experience, not generic web controls placed over unrelated scenery. Keep catalog lookup and selection state in the UI layer, not in Three.js. If the renderer fails, retain usable accessible controls with a graceful visual fallback; this fallback is not permission to omit the Three.js scene from the normal experience.

When Play is confirmed, pass the selected chart and its paired recording/segment into the existing gameplay/audio setup. `createAudioTransport` decodes the recording, then rejects it if `buffer.duration < sourceStart + duration`; this check happens during load, before transport becomes ready. Longer decoded audio is accepted, but the transport still plays only the configured segment. Preserve that contract: catalog validation must not round, clamp, pad, or otherwise rewrite `ChartConfig.sourceStart` or `duration`. A shortfall cannot start unchanged and must be surfaced as a content/transport issue—not silently shortened, clamped, or padded. For a full track, zero source offset and the transport's lower-bound check are necessary but not sufficient to show that the chart covers the whole decoded recording. Before integration, measure actual positive decoded-minus-chart residuals for the six recordings across supported browsers/sample rates; document whether they are harmless metadata/sample rounding or MP3 padding, versus omitted song content. Do not invent a numerical tolerance. Unexpected residuals or shortfalls require review rather than silent timing changes. Do not let a stale transport, chart, renderer projection, or UI title from the previous selection leak into a new run. Selecting or returning to another song must not reuse the old song's score or run state.

## Edge cases and non-goals

- Empty catalog or empty collection: explain that no songs are available; preserve an All Songs/back route.
- Search/filter with no matches: show a clear empty result and an easy way to clear filters; do not auto-start or replace the query.
- All results unavailable: retain visible rows and explain why Play is unavailable without inventing unlock requirements.
- Unsupported/stale difficulty: keep it only if the selected chart supports it; otherwise use that chart's `defaultDifficulty()`. With no selected song, clear run difficulty and disable Play. Never pass an invalid difficulty into game creation.
- Missing chart/audio, malformed chart, decoded duration below `sourceStart + duration`, unexpected full-track residual, or load/decode failure: report a recoverable song-specific error; no silent MVP substitution, truncation, padding, or timing adjustment.
- Favorite storage unavailable/corrupt: keep browsing functional and fall back to session-only favorites with a non-blocking notice.
- More than 100 songs: list remains scrollable with all entries represented; search and filters operate on the complete filtered catalog, not only rendered/visible rows. Keep list virtualization an implementation choice driven by measured performance, not a contract requirement.
- This document does not define chart authoring/verification procedure, audio preview behavior, scores/personal-best persistence, unlocks, currencies, progression, rankings, cloud sync, or the detailed transition lifecycle assigned to `.7.4`.

## Validation contract

**Planned isolated contract tests** should cover registry validation (duplicate/unknown IDs, chart/audio pairing, segment checks, supported/default difficulties, collection references), deterministic ordering, search normalization and scope, combined filters, favorite toggling by stable ID, selection when filters change, and Play eligibility. With stub decoded durations, assert the current transport boundary: exact `sourceStart + duration` and longer buffers pass its duration check; a duration just below that bound and any further shortfall reject without changing chart timing. Assert each of the six current candidate charts preserves `twilightAt === duration / 2` exactly, and assert First Shining Star's excerpt source offset, duration, and authored Twilight boundary remain unchanged. Use mixed Normal/Hard and Easy-only fixtures to assert the difficulty filter is independent from run difficulty; after filter changes assert result computation, stable-ID retention/removal, deterministic first-available selection or clear, then retain-supported/default run-difficulty reconciliation. Verify no transition can produce a run difficulty unsupported by the selected chart. Tests must not treat schema-valid generated charts as musically verified.

**Planned audio evidence before integration (not yet performed):** decode all six candidate recordings in supported browsers and record decoded durations/sample rates against authored chart intervals. Verify exact-boundary acceptance; document observed positive residuals, distinguish harmless metadata/sample rounding or MP3 padding from omitted song content, and identify unacceptable extra content or any shortfall. Confirm records accepted by the boundary reach transport ready and can start with chart/audio timing unchanged. This is implementation evidence required before integrating the full-track candidates, not a test result or blocker to approving the catalog design. Do not turn observations into a numerical tolerance without review.

**Actual-app checks** are also required: compare the rendered overview and browser with both exact approved images, including the overview's selected-collection panel and Browse Collection activation, the browser's breadcrumb/back path, song detail, difficulty, and Play; retain their Observatory visual treatment rather than using generic controls over scenery. Navigate via All Songs without the map; search and favorite entries; scroll past the first visible rows in a 100+ fixture; browse a curated collection and return; select every currently registered real song and confirm displayed title, chart, audio, allowed difficulty, source offset, and duration stay paired; exercise unavailable/candidate states, invalid/missing data, empty results, audio decode failure/retry, and returning to selection. Verify selector controls remain usable at keyboard/mobile widths. Do not call hypothetical scores/unlocks real data, and do not call any generated chart—including the current First Shining Star excerpt—musically verified without human review.

## Decisions and open questions

**Approved baseline:** explicit static registry; stable IDs; chart-owned timing; one initial constellation containing all seven current songs, with its name/story left to curation; direct All Songs and search; browser-local favorites; generated candidates playable pending refinement; First Shining Star remains an explicitly labeled demo excerpt with full-song chart work tracked as `.4f4.7.6`; current default-difficulty helper; separate availability and chart confidence; deliberate Three.js-plus-HTML/SVG hybrid; no inferred unlock or score behavior.

**Still open:** names and story copy for the initial constellation, future curated groupings and membership, preview behavior, and score/progression rules. Full-song First Shining Star is decided product intent; authoring and verifying a full-song chart and compatible recording interval is follow-up `.4f4.7.6`. Full-track decoded-duration residuals remain unmeasured; document and review actual browser evidence before integration without inventing a tolerance or changing chart timing. This is an implementation evidence prerequisite, not a blocker to approving the catalog design. Do not promote the current First Shining Star excerpt to full-song content by changing only a duration field.
