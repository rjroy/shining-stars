# Brightvale asset context

This is project-specific guidance for asset briefs, not approval to create a particular asset. Consult current lore when a brief depends on details beyond this summary.

- Visual references live in `.lore/work/mockups/` (repository-root-relative): `Golden City Rhythm Concert.webp` and `Neon Twilight Rhythm City.webp`. Open these images when developing briefs, concepts, and model critiques. Use them for visual quality, composition, palette, and atmosphere; the current approved design and amendments below govern which elements to retain or exclude.

- The approved perspective-highway direction is `.lore/work/design/brightvale-perspective-highway.md`. Its later user amendment removes both stage and performer and forbids a replacement focal structure; this **supersedes** the earlier stage/performer proposal in that same document. Keep city and crowd. Make the highway and readable gameplay cues primary.
- Preserve a low, dramatic viewpoint, a centered highway dominating the foreground, clear lanes and compact crisp note bars with restrained halos. Crop side scenery before compressing lanes. Twilight remains perpetual: warm Audience and violet/darker Twilight are expressions of twilight, not day/night.
- The approved document's lane, timing-band, camera, and responsive-fit details are gameplay/rendering constraints, not permission to change mechanics or to claim an unvalidated fit. Do not import decorative mockup details (score, stars, combo, lane emblems, enclosing note panel) as requirements.
- `.lore/work/design/brightvale-mvp-experience.md` is explicitly a draft; its older stage/performer and visual details are provisional and are not current authority over the later approved amendment. `.lore/work/design/brightvale-mvp-technical.md` is also a draft. Treat both as context, not approvals.

For each proposed asset, cite the actual current source documents in its brief and flag any conflict for the user instead of choosing silently. Reusable modeling/rendering techniques belong in the workflow skills; do not mix generic technique advice into this project-direction record.
